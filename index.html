About LockHabit

We build practical, trustworthy tools that help people make smarter money decisions without friction. No logins required. Inputs can be saved locally on your device, or you can email yourself a summary. Our long-term vision is a comprehensive, global calculator hub that’s fast, accurate, and easy to use.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without sign-up. You can opt to save inputs locally or send results to your email.

Is this available outside the U.S.?

Yes. Most tools are global and we’re adding region-specific logic over time. A live currency converter is included.

Is my data private?

We keep things simple: local device storage by default, clear reset options, and no selling of personal data.
