# Compound Interest: The Calm Way to Build Wealth

Compound interest is interest that earns interest. When your balance grows, the new, larger balance earns even more next period.

## Why compounding works
- Your money grows *exponentially*, not linearly.
- Time is the multiplier. The earlier you start, the less you need per month.

### Simple example
Start with `$1,000` at `5%` per year:
- Year 1: $1,050
- Year 2: $1,102.50
- Year 10: $1,628.89

> The money you add + the time you stay invested matter more than finding a perfect return.

## Practical steps
1. Automate a monthly transfer.
2. Prefer low-cost, diversified funds.
3. Increase by 1–2% each raise.

*Compounding is slow at first, then unstoppable. Start small, stay consistent.*
