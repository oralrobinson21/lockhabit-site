# Emergency Funds: How Much You Need (and How to Get There)

An emergency fund covers real life: layoffs, medical bills, surprise repairs. It keeps you from using credit cards at the worst moment.

## How much to save
- Starter: **3 months** of essential expenses.
- Standard: **6 months**.
- Max safety: **9–12 months** if income is variable.

## Build it simply
- Pick a number (expenses × months).
- Auto-save weekly.
- Park it in a high-yield account.

> Your emergency fund is permission to breathe. It buys you time to make good decisions.
