BUDGETING

Starter budget builder

Build a first-pass budget in a few sliders. Then tweak it until it feels realistic, not perfect, so you finally have a plan you can actually live with.

Tool • Coming soon Designed for: people who’ve tried budgeting before and bounced off.

What this tool will do

You’ll set a quick, realistic budget without needing a spreadsheet:

  • Enter your monthly take-home income
  • Set sliders for rent, food, transit, debt, and fun
  • See how much is left for savings or catch-up
  • Save a simple “budget snapshot” you can revisit

Tool status

We’re designing a slider-based layout that feels more like a game than homework.

While we build it, you can use the Monthly money map logic: list income at the top, then rough percentages for rent, food, transit, debt, and savings. The calculator will turn that into a clean, shareable view.
