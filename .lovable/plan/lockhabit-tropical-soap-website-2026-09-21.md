# LOCKHABIT Tropical Soap Website

## Goal
Create a polished one-page storefront for LOCKHABIT that feels warm, bright, tropical, clean, and inviting, using the uploaded photo as visual inspiration rather than placing it directly on the page.

## Page structure
- Compact navigation with the LOCKHABIT wordmark and links to Shop, Ingredients, and Story
- Full-width opening section featuring original tropical coconut-soap imagery, a concise product promise, price, and prominent “Shop now” button
- Featured soap range with Coconut Beach as the lead product and a small set of complementary tropical scents
- Ingredient-focused section highlighting coconut, botanical oils, and small-batch care
- Short brand story with an airy beach-inspired composition
- Closing purchase prompt and minimal footer

## Look and motion
- Sunlit ivory, ocean turquoise, leafy green, coconut brown, and warm coral accents
- Editorial display type paired with a clean, friendly body type
- Crisp layouts, restrained corners, generous breathing room, and vivid product photography
- Gentle entrance motion, subtle drifting botanical details, and tactile product/button interactions
- Motion will remain restrained and respect reduced-motion settings
- Mobile and desktop layouts will preserve clear reading order and comfortable controls

## Shopping behavior
- “Shop now” and product actions will scroll to or select the featured product area
- A lightweight cart drawer will allow adding products, changing quantities, and seeing a total
- Checkout will be presented as a polished demo interaction; no live payment processing or stored orders are included in this first version

## Content assumptions
- Use the LOCKHABIT name and Coconut Beach Soap details visible in the reference image
- Create tasteful placeholder copy, scent names, and prices for the additional products
- Generate original tropical product visuals so the uploaded reference remains inspiration only

## Final checks
- Confirm the full page, cart interactions, navigation, and animations work
- Review both desktop and mobile layouts for legibility, spacing, and overlap
- Add unique page title and social-sharing description for LOCKHABIT
