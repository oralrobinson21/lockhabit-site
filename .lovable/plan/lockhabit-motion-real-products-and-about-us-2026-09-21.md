# LOCKHABIT motion, real products, and About Us

## Goal
Fix the two visible motion breaks first, replace the generated product mockups with the supplied real product photography, and add a polished standalone About Us page using the supplied copy and portraits.

## 1. Repair the top banner
- Rebuild the moving coral notice strip as two identical, measured groups instead of translating one loosely spaced row by `50%`.
- Keep one group entering as the other exits so there is never a blank section, disappearance, or jump at any screen width.
- Preserve the current offers, colors, speed, and placement.
- Pause motion only for reduced-motion visitors while keeping the full message visible.

## 2. Replace the broken ocean loop
- Replace the current hero source with a new seamless coastal cycle designed around matching first and last frames.
- Motion sequence: water advances, recedes fully, advances again, recedes again, and settles on the same shoreline state where it began.
- Keep the coconut soap, palm canopy, rocks, shoreline, lighting, label, shape, and proportions stable; only the palms and water move.
- Remove stop-start timing, frozen beats, mid-wave cuts, visible rewinds, label warping, and sudden speed changes.
- Keep autoplay muted and inline, with the existing still image for reduced-motion visitors.
- Review every other repeating animation on home and product pages so none visibly snaps or vanishes.

## 3. Use the real products everywhere
- Extract the four clean product views from each of the twelve supplied PDF sheets.
- Match every view to the correct product and preserve the real packaging, label text, bar color, and proportions.
- Replace generated mockups in homepage cards, featured gallery, product galleries, related-product cards, and bag thumbnails.
- Keep each product’s current label art as supporting imagery only where useful; the real packaged product becomes the primary image.
- Use consistent crops and neutral cleanup so the catalog looks cohesive without altering the products.

## 4. Add a standalone About Us page
- Create `/about` and add it to the main menu and existing company/footer link without changing the rest of the footer.
- Opening interaction: a beachside management memo unrolls downward and reveals “About Us,” then the full supplied copy in clear sections:
  - We Opened a Soap Company Instead
  - A Memo from The Management
  - How the Soap Gets Made
  - Meet the Management
  - What We Believe
- Use the supplied Daisy photo and Oral portrait.
- Give Daisy a tasteful daisy-flower accent/crown while preserving her real appearance.
- Present Oral with a restrained tropical operations treatment that fits the management joke without changing his identity.
- Stage both portraits around the memo, not inside nested cards.
- Animate the shoreline behind the paper with a smooth advance-and-recede cycle that returns exactly to its opening position; provide a still state for reduced motion.
- Keep the page warm, bright, tropical, playful, and consistent with the existing LOCKHABIT design.

## 5. Finish and verify
- Add unique About-page metadata and preserve existing page metadata.
- Check desktop and mobile for banner continuity, readable memo text, stable portraits, correct product matching, and no overlaps.
- Watch multiple complete cycles of the homepage ocean, About shoreline, ticker, and product-page motion to confirm seamless returns.
- Test product links, About navigation, galleries, Add to bag, cart imagery, and checkout entry after the image swap.
