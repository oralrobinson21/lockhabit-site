## LOCKHABIT Media / Journal source of truth

For any task involving the Journal, blog, article writing, editorial UX, newsletters, reader accounts, comments, community, sourcing, read-aloud, creator/personality pages, or optional supporter membership, read and follow `docs/LOCKHABIT_MEDIA_OS.md` before making changes.

Key default: when the owner says “write a blog” or “write an article,” treat `docs/LOCKHABIT_MEDIA_OS.md` as the operating brief unless the owner explicitly overrides it. Preserve the fun resort/editorial identity, source factual wellness claims, keep editorial access free, and do not turn the Journal into a generic corporate blog.
