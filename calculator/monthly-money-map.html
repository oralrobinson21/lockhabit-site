PAYCHECK PLANNING

Monthly money map

Line up your pay dates with rent, bills, debt payments, and savings goals so you always know which check covers what—before the month starts.

Tool • Coming soon Designed for: people paid every week, every 2 weeks, or twice a month.

What this tool will do

Instead of guessing “Can I afford this?” each week, you’ll see a calm, visual map of:

  • Your pay dates for the month
  • Major bills (rent, car, phone, childcare, debt)
  • Everyday categories like groceries, gas, and fun
  • How much is left each check for savings or catching up

Think of it like a GPS for your paychecks.

Tool status

We’re building a simple, slider-based version of this calculator. You’ll be able to:

  • Pick how often you’re paid
  • Enter your take-home pay per check
  • Add your main bills and their due dates
  • See a line-by-line map of each paycheck and what it covers

Until the interactive version is ready, you can still sketch a quick money map using paper or notes—one row per paycheck, one column per bill. The tool will automate that.

While we finish the tool

We’ll quietly email you when this calculator goes live.
